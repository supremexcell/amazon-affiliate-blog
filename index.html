Who this is for

You play on PC, PS5, Xbox, or Switch, you need a working mic for Discord / party chat, and you do not want to spend $100. Budget: roughly $30–50 in the US, £25–50 in the UK.

How I compared them — honesty note

I did not claim a 2-year hands-on test of all three. This is built from published specs, lab notes (RTINGS names the Logitech G432 its best budget gaming headset tested), and Amazon customer-review patterns as of November 2026. Where I write “from the specs / from reviews”, that is what it is. Prices are ranges, not live Amazon prices.

What you do not get under $50: real low-latency wireless, active noise cancelling, or a studio-grade mic. Every headset below is wired. That is normal — and wired is the right call here.

Which one should you buy?

  • Most people, most platforms → Stinger 2.
  • PC only, EQ, footsteps matter → G432 (worst earpads — that is the trade).
  • Hate heavy headsets → Kraken X.
  • Glasses + 4+ hours → Stinger 2 first, Kraken X second. Avoid the G432 unless you can try it.

What I would NOT buy under $50

Any “wireless gaming headset” under $50 — real low-latency wireless has not cracked this price yet. And no-name headsets with 12 reviews promising “7.1 + noise cancelling + RGB” for $15 — the mic is what breaks first.
